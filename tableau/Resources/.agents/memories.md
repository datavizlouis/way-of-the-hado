# Memories

- **External origins:** the site's allowed-origins list was empty when the app was scaffolded, so nothing external can load. Every image, font and sprite is inlined as a data URI in `hado-assets.js` (data URIs also keep canvases untainted for WebGL); audio ships as files and plays through `<audio>`. Don't route `<audio>` through WebAudio: cross-origin media turns silent.
- **No viewport units:** inside a dashboard the iframe is as tall as the zone, so the page scrolls in the parent and `vh` is meaningless. Section heights are fixed pixels. `IntersectionObserver` still reports what's on screen and is used to pause off-screen animation.
- **Artwork:** the user supplied Super SF2 sprite sheets (Lord Zymeth), select portraits (Phatman) and select-screen parts (GaryCXJK) for a non-commercial project, and wants them credited. The footer credits them, plus Deekman for Akuma's sprites. A Doug So Retro sheet of Akuma dialogue portraits was also supplied (raw/akuma_portraits_dougsoretro.png), currently unused. The images and everything cut from them stay out of git; only the scripts and mappings are committed. The music is still a placeholder.
- **Logo and brush style (SF4 reference):** the user picked Cafe Felix (JSHcreates, personal use, git-ignored) as the brush font and the Street Fighter IV logo/OST cover as the style. The logo is drawn in code (`app/src/lib/logo.js`): no paper strokes on the title; one big black ink splat holds HADO with room to spare; HADO is yellow-orange-red brush lettering with a ragged black brush border and flame flicks; "WAY OF THE" is plain white above it with no splat. A glowing red dot sits in HADO's A with red energy swirling round it and embers rising (`app/src/lib/aura.js`, Akuma's Satsui no Hado as the reference), drawn half behind and half in front of the letters. Brush strokes carry sumi-e splatter (edge bleed, flecks, spray, landing splash, end flick) in `app/src/lib/brush.js`. Cafe Felix's free build shows a watermark for digits and punctuation, so the font is limited to letters via `unicode-range`.
- **The user's select-screen preferences:** borderless, player 1 only, no full-body sprite next to the portrait, no volume or mute controls, music changes automatically with the selected fighter, and text optically centred on the equaliser.
- **Roster change:** Balrog's second move is Dash Uppercut, because Turn Punch isn't on his sheet.
- **Franchise figures (rebuilt 2026-11):** the user's rule is no invented numbers: every figure traces to an official source (publisher IR, fact books, press releases, or an official post relayed by a named outlet) or an established analyst firm. Wikipedia, fan wikis and VGChartz don't qualify. `data/franchise_metrics.csv` keeps only the latest qualifying figure per series and title, plus box office (Box Office Mojo, The Numbers) and three sourced Street Fighter II quotes (Capcom IR history, RePlay magazine). No lifetime revenue per franchise exists from any qualifying source (the SF2 "$10.6B" and MK "$5B" don't trace), so the user chose sourced data only: no revenue charts. KOF, Fatal Fury and Samurai Shodown have no figure at all and show "No published figure"; Smash and Guilty Gear have no series total and show "≥" the sum of their named titles. Mortal Kombat's 80M (May 2023) predates MK1, so MK1 is drawn past the bar, not added. Brawl uses Nintendo's verified 13.15M (2016); the current page couldn't be loaded. No qualifying source was found for SF2 cabinets in convenience stores or petrol stations, so the story doesn't claim it.
- **Credits (user request, SF2 win screen reference):** `Credits.jsx` replaces the old sources screen and footer. Left: the beaten mugshot (third frame of each fighter's mugshot panel on the raw sheets, `img/portraits/<id>-lose.png`; `build_atlas.py` merges overlapping pieces because Fei Long's fist slices separately). Right: a win quote, cycling independently at random every 2 s. The user chose a mix of SF2 and Super SF2 quotes (option c); `lib/winQuotes.js` holds 31 verbatim lines from the Street Fighter Wiki Quotes pages (Ryu's also checked on Wikipedia), each arcade line listed once, with wording conflicts and unversioned lines left out. Below: Data Sources (full width, from the metrics rows), Asset Sources and Copyright (half width each), all in yellow 3px frames. Fits one screen at 1366x768 to 1920x1080.
- **Move sounds (user supplied, `app/assets/audio/special moves/`, git-ignored with the rest of the audio):** each named after its move, set as `sfx` in `moveTimeline.js`: Hadoken, Shoryuken, Tatsumaki, Hyakuretsukyaku, Spinning Bird Kick, Sonic Boom, Sumo Headbutt, Yoga Fire, Yoga Flame, Psycho Crusher. Sagat's Tiger Shot is `sagat-tiger`; his Tiger Uppercut is `sagat-tiger` then `sagat-uppercut`, chained on the audio clock (`playSfx` takes a list). The build names a sound by its file name wherever it sits under `audio/`. Hyakuretsukyaku plays its 0.47s sound three times back to back, one per round of kicks. Spinning Bird Kick loops its upside-down spin (frames 7-10) three times at rate 1.25, about 1.6s like its sound. Tatsumaki now loops its air spin (frames 2-5) three times, at rate 0.9, about as long as its sound.
- **Scrolling (user request):** on Meet the Street Fighters, while a fighter is zoomed in the wheel moves to the previous / next fighter in roster order, wrapping round (`Characters.jsx`; `FieldScene.select` stops the fighter being left); with nobody selected the page's one-screen scroll takes it to the story or Player Select. Every screen but the credits carries the intro's pulsing hint (`ScrollHint.jsx`): "Scroll for more", or "Scroll to change characters" while zoomed in. The credits, the last screen, say "Back to top" instead, and scrolling down there (wheel, Page Down or Down arrow) glides back to the top of the page; scrolling up goes back one screen as usual. Whenever the intro comes back on screen after leaving it (up from the fighters, or back to top from the credits) it resets to the logo beat and paints it in fresh. "Left" means under 2% visible, because a smooth scroll's last visibility report can land just above zero.
- **Data charts draw in (user request):** every time a data step is entered, scrolling down or back up, its chart resets with no animation and then draws: bars grow from zero row by row (`ROW_STAGGER` 60 ms; butterfly `BAR_STAGGER` 90 ms, the games side growing leftwards from the names), treemap boxes pop in one at a time, biggest first (`POP_STAGGER` 70 ms), and labels fade in after. `Scrolly` counts entries (`visit`) so returning to the same step redraws; `useDrawn(key)` holds the empty state for two frames. This replaced the earlier morphs between steps. Labels fade out in 0.12 s so they don't ghost over the next chart.
- **Desktop layout only (user request):** the narrow-screen ("mobile") layout was removed: the 760px and 980px media queries in `styles.css` and the stacked story-beat layout in `Intro.jsx`. The workbook itself has no phone layout to remove: it's a single worksheet, and Tableau device layouts belong to dashboards. The 1499px rule for the credits' source columns is part of the desktop layout and stays.
- **Select-screen portraits:** GaryCXJK's sheet shows its magenta background (192, 0, 192) through gaps enclosed by the art (Chun-Li's hair, Cammy's braid, Dee Jay's thumb, inside letters). `build_ui_assets.py` now clears every magenta pixel in the named portraits, not only the background touching the edge; that colour never appears in the art itself.
- **Data tooltips** close on any scroll or wheel of the page: the chart changes under a still pointer, so no mouseleave fires.
- **Story order:** the chapters run chronologically (IV, then V, then III, then 6), not in release order.

- **Story beats (intro chapters):** 7 beats (the user removed Third Strike and SF6). No brush strokes: each beat sits on a big white ink splat with art on one side and text on the other, alternating sides. The art is animated (`app/src/lib/beatScenes.js`, chosen per beat in `storyCast.js`): 1 Ryu and Ken's heavy punches meeting in a fist bump (Ken mirrored); 2 Akuma's Raging Demon win pose (stance, stomp, body goes black in a red aura with 天 above him and blue rings), cut from Deekman's sheet by `scripts/build_akuma.py` (falls back to 天 in the red aura without it); 3 Ryu's Shoryuken, Sagat hit and K.O.'d; 4 Bison's Psycho Crusher across and back; 5 a night-street homage to the SF2 attract intro (crowd, one punch, camera tilts up a tower to "THE WORLD WARRIOR"), cut to an ink-splat shape rather than a framed rectangle; 6 the S.I.N. emblem the user supplied with its red background removed (`scripts/build_story_art.py` -> `img/story/6.png`, git-ignored); 7 a procedural Black Moon (hex plates popping in and out, slow rotation, violet core). Extra sprite animations are named `beat:*` in `scripts/sprite_map.json`. The user said to ignore the phone layout for now.
- **Second design pass (user feedback):** story beats use one big white splat (`makeBlob` in `brush.js`) with few loose drops; the chapter dots are a horizontal row of white-blue orbs under it, the current one a flying Hadoken, with a pulsing "Scroll for more" above. The Fighters title is "Meet the" in white over "Street Fighters" in HADO's fire lettering (`renderFireText` in `logo.js`). Fighters are twice their old world size; hit-testing ignores transparent sprite pixels so the back row is clickable. The bio sits on a calm, even cream splat (`SplatPanel.jsx`). The select screen has a 2x2 "?" tile left of Ryu, selected by default, playing `audio/theme.mp3`; no such file yet, so it plays a chiptune. The portrait column is a fixed 142px so hovering doesn't re-centre the row.
- **One screen at a time (user request):** every section is exactly one window tall (`.screen`, 100vh) and the wheel or Page keys move one screen (`useOneScreenScroll` in `App.jsx`; CSS snap handles touch). The workbook is a single maximized worksheet, so the frame is the view's size. This replaces the original 5000-7000px long-form idea. The franchise section is now two scroll pages (`Scrolly` in `Franchise.jsx`): a sticky chart under full-screen steps, each step a snap point. Sections have `overflow: hidden`, which makes them scroll boxes and breaks both sticky and snap, so `.fx-page` uses `overflow: clip`. The bar style follows the Super SF2 Turbo HUD the user sent, simplified at their request: no portrait tile, no red track, each frame only as long as its value, and "No published figure" frames trimmed to the text. Second pass (user feedback): page 1 is franchise view first (one bar each, only Street Fighter coloured, the rest light grey; title "Street Fighter claims 3rd" on one line), then the game view (all grey until hovered; the hovered game lifts). Page 2 dropped the box-office bars: SF treemap all in the HUD yellow with SF2 ringed in a pulsing red border ("Street Fighter's Legacy" / "The Arcade Boom"), films treemap with SF yellow and the rest blue ("Hollywood takes a swing" / "Fatality. MK wins."), then a butterfly (games left, box office right). Card copy is conversational with no source footers; the sources screen lists them. Tooltips: big value, eyebrow, blank line, then the row's Note, which is written in plain words for this purpose. Note "claims 3rd" ranks only series with an official total: Smash's four reported games alone (66.7M) also beat Street Fighter, and the copy says so. The sources and credits screen follows; Page 3 (credits) is to come. The intro keeps the bottom 110px clear for the carousel.
- **Music:** `lib/player.js` is the one site-wide player: the selected track loops everywhere, from its VGM loop point (`trackLoops.js`), and never stops on scroll. `opening` plays first on every load, but browsers hold sound until the first click or key press. The WAVs come from `scripts/convert_music.py`, which needs VGMPlay because the tracks use QSound and YM2151 + OKIM6295, which ffmpeg's libgme can't play. `player_select.vgz` was supplied but has no slot yet.
- **Music changes on click only:** hovering or arrow keys move the cursor and map; a click, Enter or Space changes the portrait and track. "Now playing" names the track that is on, not the fighter under the cursor.
- **Music format:** the WAVs stay in `app/assets/audio` as masters; the site ships 192 kbps constant-bitrate MP3s (`scripts/convert_music.py --mp3`), because the WAVs would add about 177 MB to the package.
- **Move animation (user feedback, replaces the shared motion curves):** every move's frame order, lift and travel live in `lib/moveTimeline.js`, keyed to frame positions, so a fighter leaves the ground exactly when the user asked (usually when the leg lifts). Nothing draws back before moving: travelling moves start from the fighter's spot and go right, and `rear: true` lines frames up on the back edge because foot-based anchors can sit on a claw or a boot. A move that ends away from home glides back afterwards. Moves the user listed as stationary have no travel at all.
- **Projectiles:** each flies left to right in its own art, cut from the sheets' projectile panels (`rects` in `sprite_map.json`, since those frames are too small for the slicer), and passes behind the bio panel. An earlier round made them red orbs flying up; that was a misreading and the user corrected it.
- **Sheet quirks found:** panels can pull in frames that belong elsewhere, so `frames` filters them: Ken's last Hadoken frame is only the fireball, Ryu's fourth has it painted on, Yoga Flame's panel includes jump kicks from the panel above, and one Hyakuretsukyaku frame sat 147px lower on the sheet, which lifted her off-screen. Cammy's Spiral Arrow is cut by `rects` because the flight frames were merged with a white divider line. `noShadow` erases shadows painted into frames of moves that leave the ground.
- **Field layout:** two zig-zag rows of 8 with no overlap at 1366, 1600 and 1920 wide (`SPACING`, `BACK_Z`, `BACK_SPREAD` and the raised camera in `overviewPose`). The back row is spread wider so perspective doesn't pull the zig-zag out of line. The user asked for fighters 1.5 to 2x bigger: about 1.5x fits edge to edge, and going further would need overlap or fewer per row.
- **Moves, second pass:** Tatsumaki, Spinning Bird Kick and Machine Gun Upper stay on the spot, because the frames already show the lift. To speed up one phase of a move (Honda, Bison and Cammy's flight, T.Hawk's dive) the timeline raises `rate` and repeats the other phases' frames in `seq`. Projectiles fade over the last 110px before the bio panel (`projectileLimit`). Ryu and Ken's Hadoken plays `hadouken.mp3` as the move starts (`sfx` in the timeline). The user is happy with Bison's Psycho Crusher skimming the floor, and with the map following hover while the portrait waits for a click; the "1P" on the cursor tile stays.
- **Player select:** hovering moves the cursor and the map; the portrait and music wait for a click, which also plays `select.mp3`. The equaliser was removed; before the music starts the line reads "Click a fighter to listen to their theme song". The now-playing line is `width: 0; min-width: 100%` so its text can't widen the centred row (it used to push the "?" portrait 56px left before the first click). The "Sound test" label, the "1P" tag beside the portrait and the footer text were removed at the user's request. The "1P" on the cursor tile stays.
- **Story beats, sound and size:** `coin` plays as the fists meet in beat 1, `akuma` when his foot lands in beat 2 (frame 5 of the sequence, not its start), `shoryuken` as Ryu's move starts in beat 3 (after his eye burns red and the pixelfx "Cursed" effect, blood palette and no skull, builds on him: `lib/cursedFx.js`, MIT, credited in the footer; the user tried a lens flare and dropped it), and `psycho_crusher` on beat 4's right-to-left crossing only (`playSfx`, via `cue` in `beatScenes.js`). They only sound after the viewer's first click. `artShare` in `storyCast.js` gives beats 3, 4 and 5 more of the width so the art can scale up. Akuma now plays his real idle cycle before the stomp.
- **Audio in Tableau:** `<audio>` fails in the extension sandbox with "NotSupportedError: The element has no supported sources", even though the MP3s are in the package. Tracks are therefore shipped as `src/audio/<id>.js` (base64, loaded with `<script src>`) and played through Web Audio in `lib/player.js`. Audio errors show on the select screen, since the frame has no visible console.
