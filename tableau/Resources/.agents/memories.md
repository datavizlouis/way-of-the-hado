# Memories

- **External origins:** the site's allowed-origins list was empty when the app was scaffolded, so nothing external can load. Every image, font and sprite is inlined as a data URI in `hado-assets.js` (data URIs also keep canvases untainted for WebGL); audio ships as files and plays through `<audio>`. Don't route `<audio>` through WebAudio: cross-origin media turns silent.
- **No viewport units:** inside a dashboard the iframe is as tall as the zone, so the page scrolls in the parent and `vh` is meaningless. Section heights are fixed pixels. `IntersectionObserver` still reports what's on screen and is used to pause off-screen animation.
- **Artwork:** the user supplied Super SF2 sprite sheets (Lord Zymeth), select portraits (Phatman) and select-screen parts (GaryCXJK) for a non-commercial project, and wants them credited. The footer credits them, plus Deekman for Akuma's sprites. A Doug So Retro sheet of Akuma dialogue portraits was also supplied (raw/akuma_portraits_dougsoretro.png), currently unused. The images and everything cut from them stay out of git; only the scripts and mappings are committed. The music is still a placeholder.
- **Logo and brush style (SF4 reference):** the user picked Cafe Felix (JSHcreates, personal use, git-ignored) as the brush font and the Street Fighter IV logo/OST cover as the style. The logo is drawn in code (`app/src/lib/logo.js`): no paper strokes on the title; one big black ink splat holds HADO with room to spare; HADO is yellow-orange-red brush lettering with a ragged black brush border and flame flicks; "WAY OF THE" is plain white above it with no splat. A glowing red dot sits in HADO's A with red energy swirling round it and embers rising (`app/src/lib/aura.js`, Akuma's Satsui no Hado as the reference), drawn half behind and half in front of the letters. Brush strokes carry sumi-e splatter (edge bleed, flecks, spray, landing splash, end flick) in `app/src/lib/brush.js`. Cafe Felix's free build shows a watermark for digits and punctuation, so the font is limited to letters via `unicode-range`.
- **The user's select-screen preferences:** borderless, player 1 only, no full-body sprite next to the portrait, no volume or mute controls, music changes automatically with the selected fighter, and text optically centred on the equaliser.
- **Roster change:** Balrog's second move is Dash Uppercut, because Turn Punch isn't on his sheet.
- **Franchise figures:** the researcher couldn't open primary pages and relied on search snippets, so many rows are marked "secondary" in the Note column. Verify against Capcom IR before sharing widely.
- **Story order:** the chapters run chronologically (IV, then V, then III, then 6), not in release order.

- **Story beats (intro chapters):** 7 beats (the user removed Third Strike and SF6). No brush strokes: each beat sits on a big white ink splat with art on one side and text on the other, alternating sides. The art is animated (`app/src/lib/beatScenes.js`, chosen per beat in `storyCast.js`): 1 Ryu and Ken's heavy punches meeting in a fist bump (Ken mirrored); 2 Akuma's Raging Demon win pose (stance, stomp, body goes black in a red aura with 天 above him and blue rings), cut from Deekman's sheet by `scripts/build_akuma.py` (falls back to 天 in the red aura without it); 3 Ryu's Shoryuken, Sagat hit and K.O.'d; 4 Bison's Psycho Crusher across and back; 5 a night-street homage to the SF2 attract intro (crowd, one punch, camera tilts up a tower to "THE WORLD WARRIOR"), cut to an ink-splat shape rather than a framed rectangle; 6 the S.I.N. emblem the user supplied with its red background removed (`scripts/build_story_art.py` -> `img/story/6.png`, git-ignored); 7 a procedural Black Moon (hex plates popping in and out, slow rotation, violet core). Extra sprite animations are named `beat:*` in `scripts/sprite_map.json`. The user said to ignore the phone layout for now.
